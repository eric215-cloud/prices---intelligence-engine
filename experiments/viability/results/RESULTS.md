## Phase 0 - baseline: real candidates promoted in the copy with no class recorded

- 59 real candidates selected (Chase Sapphire Reserve packet, Visa Infinite baselines, two loyalty candidates). Promoted with no class recorded for their conditions: 58 refused (CONDITIONS_NOT_CLASSIFIED), 1 accepted because they state no restriction and no open question, 1 refused by the evidence policy.
- Before the safety correction every one of them was accepted, and for an invented cardholder whose account was opened in 2024 the report listed the welcome offer, IHG Platinum, Priority Pass and five more as established (experiments/safety/before_after.py re-runs that on the earlier commit).
- Restrictions on the 73 real non-transfer candidates: 124 strings. Engineering's classification: G 24, M 9, T 11, U 68, g 12.
- (U usage term, G gate not checked by the rule, g gate that is checked, T time window, M research note)
- Candidates that state a gate their rule does not check: 20 of 73; 19 of them derive an entitlement.
