Benefits for Marriott Bonvoy members after linking accounts:
- Member: 2,000 FLY ON Points each year
- Silver Elite: 5,000
- Gold Elite: 10,000
- Platinum Elite: 20,000
- Titanium Elite: 30,000, and Crystal status or higher based on accumulated FLY ON Points
- Ambassador Elite: 40,000, and Crystal status or higher based on accumulated FLY ON Points
One JAL account can be linked to one Marriott account. Once linked it cannot be changed.
