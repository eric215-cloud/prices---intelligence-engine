   - Southwest Rapid Rewards **A-List**: link account; 10–15 business days to activate; through Dec 31 of following year.
   - **$250 Shops at Chase credit**: qualifying purchases only; up to $500 in simultaneously available credits after consecutive qualifications; typically automatic.
   - **$500 Southwest Airlines Chase Travel credit**: qualifying Southwest purchase through Chase Travel only; credited amount does not earn UR; up to $1,000 simultaneously across consecutive qualifications; typically automatic.

   All require **$75,000 eligible card purchases in Jan–Dec**, including authorized-user purchases but excluding cash-like transactions, P2P, gambling and fees. A downgrade followed by an upgrade may erase earlier annual qualifying spend. Allow 1–2 weeks for milestone recognition. **Eric's personal progress and qualification are unknown.** Primary-only status recipients differ from AU spend inclusion.
