## Findings most likely to affect real decisions

1. **2027 redesign is material.** Pre-2027 cardholders can have a Flexible Bilt Cash
   option and the annual $200 Bilt Cash + $400 portal-hotel-credit structure. Their
   first card anniversary on/after January 1, 2027 transitions those benefits to
   $600 annual Bilt Cash unless a timely legacy election preserves the former
   structure. There is no basis to assert which election Eric has made.
2. **Rent/mortgage earning is conditional**, not simply 1.25x. Housing-only tiers
   depend on monthly qualifying everyday-to-housing spend. In the pre-2027 Flex
   option, $3 Bilt Cash unlocks 100 housing points, capped at 1x.
3. **Bilt Cash and Bilt Points are different.** Cash cannot simply be taken as a
   statement credit. The Point Accelerator requires spending $200 Cash for extra
   1x on up to $5,000 selected-card spend; legacy Flex eligibility may matter.
4. **Priority Pass terms are specific.** Eligible cardholder + two guests; issuer
   enrollment; Bilt app QR available without separate activation. Physical card
   and Priority Pass app use require a dedicated activated membership. Additional
   guests cost $35. Lounge capacity and individual lounge rules still apply.
5. **Transfer directory is count-inconsistent.** The live Bilt Rewards webpage
   names 26 external programs but advertises 27; its FAQ says 23. The 26 records
   are verified *named connections*, with each individual exchange ratio left null.
6. **Architecture needs restraint.** Column N.A. is the issuer. Cardless is an
   express servicer; its formal program-manager role is a qualified functional
   mapping. The economic capital provider is not evidenced.
